About Our Team

The Thomas Curtiss Real Estate Team, Keller Williams, serving Hanford, Lemoore, and beyond. Photos are the public portraits from the current site.

Thomas Curtiss, MBA

Founder, Visionary, and Forward-Thinking Leader
DRE 01928941

Thomas Curtiss, MBA, is the founder and driving force behind the Thomas Curtiss Real Estate Team. He has closed and overseen more than 1,000 real estate transactions and leads from a framework he calls the 5 F's: Faith, Family, Fitness, Finances, and Future. A California licensed salesperson (DRE 01928941) and Team Leader with Keller Williams, he has led the team since 2020. Thomas focuses on building systems, developing leaders, and helping clients build a life they love, one home at a time.

Josephine Curtiss

COO, Execution Leader, and Operational Excellence

Josephine is the COO of the Thomas Curtiss Real Estate Team and the person who holds the operation together. She oversees the major functions of the organization and is known for her ability to execute, implement, and elevate every moving part of the business so clients get a seamless experience. She has led Legacy Leavers as CEO since 2016, and she previously served as a personal lines account manager at Mackey and Mackey Insurance Agency in Hanford. Under her guidance, the team works with clarity, structure, and excellence.

Laura Goldsmith

Team Leader, Broker, and Life-Transition Specialist
DRE 02051157

Laura Goldsmith is Team Leader and Broker for the Thomas Curtiss Real Estate Team (DRE 02051157).
